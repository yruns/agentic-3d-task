# Research Reports

This chapter contains literature reviews and research planning documents.
Reports distinguish published results, public implementation details, and
proposed research questions. They are not benchmark execution records.

## Research Point Decisions

- [研究点3：面向连续多任务的室内具身巡航（选题记录）](research_point3_multitask_cruise_20261005.md):
  the confirmed direction for research point 3 (2026-10-05): continuous
  multi-task indoor navigation and question answering with persistent memory,
  where research point 1 serves as the online memory retrieval interface and
  research point 2 provides instance disambiguation, verification, and
  answering. Records the rejected alternatives, benchmark comparison with
  citation counts, work items, evaluation plan, stage gate, timeline, and
  risks. It supersedes the research point 3 recommendations in the 2026-10-03
  reports below.

## Reports

- [通用大模型在室内三维任务中的能力与适用边界](indoor_3d_llm_capability_survey_20261003.md):
  a cross-task evidence review of question answering, grounding, functional
  understanding, memory, planning, scene generation, and spatial reasoning;
  distinguishes useful model capabilities from complete task reliability and
  develops the third research topic around long-horizon embodied task execution
  (that recommendation is superseded by the 2026-10-05 decision record).
- [冻结通用大模型的室内巡航导航研究](zero_shot_indoor_llm_navigation_20261003.md):
  a navigation-focused review prioritizing zero-shot navigation with general
  LLMs/VLMs and optional small-module training; includes revised baselines,
  training and sensor boundaries, and public benchmark choices.
- [基于多模态大模型的室内长程视觉语言导航研究报告](indoor_long_horizon_vln_survey_20261003.md):
  task definitions, benchmark protocols, representative methods, recent progress,
  reproducibility limits, and thesis research opportunities, reviewed through
  2026-10-03.
