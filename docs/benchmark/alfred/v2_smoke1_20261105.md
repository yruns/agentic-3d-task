## Observed failure and retained evidence

All 10 attempted simulator actions succeeded. The actor observed beds and a desk
lamp but had not detected a pillow. It repeatedly rotated and retrieved known
bed views before requesting navigation. The failure was an output-contract
problem: the schema exposed two independently nullable target fields while the
application required exactly one. Repeating the same schema at finalization
did not communicate a structurally enforceable choice.
