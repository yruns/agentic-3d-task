# v8.1 — full 20-case online run on `421254` (2026-07-03)

First multi-sample online run of the Phase-2c agent-confirmed **2D SeedConfirmation**
contract (see [v8](v8_seed_confirmation_online_20260703.md) for the contract
itself). Where v8 was a single cherry-checked smoke (IoU 0.72 on one clean
drawer-knob sample), v8.1 runs **20 samples end-to-end** through the real
ModelHub adapter + Molmo/SAM sidecars and scores every one. This is the first
decision-relevant (though single-scene) accuracy signal for the online contract.

**Headline:** 20/20 samples completed and scored (0 failures after retries).
**Mean IoU 0.182, AP25 0.25 (5/20), AP50 0.05 (1/20)** — high per-sample
variance (best 0.545, six cases at 0.0). The mean is far below v8's n=1 smoke
(0.72), i.e. the smoke sample was not representative; per-sample over-selection
and wrong-seed selection remain the accuracy bottleneck (Phase 2c fixed the
*contract*, not accuracy tuning — that is the offline fusion sweep / future work).

**Detailed reasoning-path visualization** of the two most successful cases
(`55b56254` bottom knob, `f35fb167` middle knob — same nightstand, same frame,
different affordance) with full-resolution stage images (keyframe finding →
Molmo → SAM → seed confirmation → visibility → per-frame Molmo/SAM/lift →

## Operational findings (important for future batch runs)

1. **`CODEX_AGENT_TURN_TIMEOUT_S` must be set for batch runs.** It defaults to
   `0.0` (disabled), so a hung agent turn blocks its worker **forever**, and
   `run_samples` waits on every future — one wedged turn prevents the batch from
   ever writing `evaluation_summary.json`. The **first launch at `--workers 4`
   wedged**: 4 simultaneous turn-starts at t=0 against the single-session adapter
   stalled 2 of them indefinitely (no timeout → no `failure.json`, threads pinned).
   Fix: re-launched with `CODEX_AGENT_TURN_TIMEOUT_S=600` (safety net; a hung turn
   is interrupted + finalized instead of pinning a worker) and **`--workers 2`**
   (staggered starts avoid the thundering-herd hang — the two cases that hung
   under `-w4` completed normally under `-w2`).
2. **Transient sidecar `BrokenPipeError`.** One case (`809a07a1`) failed mid-
   pipeline on `molmo/v1/point` with `BrokenPipeError` (the intermittent remote
   proxy flakiness also seen in v7); it **succeeded on retry**. The per-frame
   Molmo/SAM calls are fail-loud (no per-frame retry), so one transient drop fails
   the whole case — a candidate for a small retry/backoff in the proposer.
3. **`--score` reads `result_kind`** and routes pipeline results to
   `score_pipeline_result_file` → `score_mask_npz`; all 20 scored cleanly.

## Path to 20/20 (fail-closed behavior worked as designed)

Main batch (`-w2`, turn-timeout 600): **17 completed, 3 failed** —
`809a07a1` (transient BrokenPipe), `c9cde8d0` (agent turn hit the 600 s timeout
after 31 tool actions and finalized with no confirmed SAM candidate →
`CodexResponseError`), `4668a5f5` (`lift_too_sparse`: confirmed seed had no
finite-positive-depth pixels). All three are **fail-closed** (no mask emitted),
consistent with the no-fallback rule. Retries:

- `809a07a1` → ✅ on retry 1 (transient).
- `c9cde8d0` → ✅ on retry 1 (agent confirmed a seed within budget).
- `4668a5f5` → ❌ retry 1 (`empty fused mask` after agreement/anchor gating),
  ✅ retry 2. This is the historically worst sample (v4/v5 over-selection:
  pred 1990 / GT 106); the agent's seed choice for it is unstable across runs.

Final: **20/20 completed + scored, 0 failures.**

## Caveats

- **Single scene (`421254`).** The local checkout has only one SceneFun3D scene,
  so this is 20 descriptions of one environment — not a cross-scene fold. Not
  comparable to published cross-scene SceneFun3D numbers.
- **High variance / accuracy is the open problem.** 6/20 at IoU 0.0 are the
  familiar failure modes: gross over-selection (`0b20f828` pred 790 vs GT 173) and
  wrong-seed / wrong-part selection. Phase 2c delivered the *contract + wiring*;
  driving IoU up needs the offline fusion sweep (`tau`/`eps`/`radius`/size-prior
  calibration, cf. v6) and better seed selection, not contract work.
- **Agent nondeterminism.** `55b56254` scored 0.545 here vs 0.717 in the v8 smoke
  (same sample, different agent seed choice). Per-sample numbers move run to run;
  the mean is the stable signal.
- **Metric denominator.** Per this archive's reporting contract, aggregates are
  over scored samples; here scored = 20 (all succeeded after retries), so no
  failures were excluded.
- **Retries were needed to reach 20/20** (2 transient + 1 hard case over 3 total
  attempts). A clean single-pass 20/20 would need proposer-level retry/backoff for
  transient sidecar drops and a more robust seed choice for the hard sample.

## Reproduce

Same CLI as above with `CODEX_AGENT_TURN_TIMEOUT_S` set and `--workers 2`
(avoid `-w4`: the t=0 thundering herd wedges the single-session adapter). Re-run
any `failure.json` cases into the same `--output-dir`, then re-score the whole
dir with `python -m codex_agent.scenefunc3d.evaluation --results-dir <run_dir>`.
